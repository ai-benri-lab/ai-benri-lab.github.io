Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-19

1. Data we access

When you connect a TikTok account with Login Kit, the Service receives an access token and, depending on the scopes you approve, may access:

  • user.info.basic – open ID, display name, avatar (to show which account is connected).
  • video.upload / video.publish – permission to upload videos created by the Service to your account.
  • video.list – the list and public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of your own videos, used to evaluate video performance.

2. How data is used

Data is used solely to publish videos to the connected account and to analyze the performance of those videos. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Access/refresh tokens and statistics are stored on a private server operated by the Service owner, on the owner's local network, with restricted access. Tokens are used only for API calls to TikTok.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account remains connected. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); revocation invalidates the token, and stored data for that account is deleted within 30 days. You may also request deletion via the contact below.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies or third-party analytics.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 18.

7. Contact

GitHub: @ai-benri-lab
